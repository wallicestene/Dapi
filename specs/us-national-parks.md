# US national parks

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_parks_of_the_United_States

One row per: national park

Columns:
- Park: the park's name, without the † or ‡ marks after it
- State: the state or territory it's in, without the coordinates; for a park in several states, all of them, separated by commas
- Established: the date it became a national park, as written
- Area (acres): the area in acres, as a number
- Visitors (2025): recreation visitors in 2025, as a number

Sample rows:

| Park | State | Established | Area (acres) | Visitors (2025) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acadia | Maine | February 26, 1919 | 49,071.40 | 4,079,318 |
| Yellowstone | Wyoming, Montana, Idaho | March 1, 1872 | 2,219,790.71 | 4,762,988 |

Rules:
- Only the main table of parks, not the "Parks by state or territory" table further down
- Every park in it: the page says how many national parks there are
- Numbers as the page writes them, with commas
