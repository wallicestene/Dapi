# Books to Scrape: travel books

Source: https://books.toscrape.com/catalogue/category/books/travel_2/index.html

One row per: book in the Travel category

Columns:
- Title: the book's full title
- Price: the price as shown, with the pound sign
- Availability: whether it's in stock, as shown
- Rating: the star rating, as a word (One to Five)
