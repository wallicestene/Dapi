## Direction contract

THESIS: Dapi's front door is an auditor's working paper for web data: every delivered value carries a tick that says what it was checked against, and the dataset is footed against the source's own total. It refuses the category default of a chat timeline beside a table with "verified" badges.

OWN-WORLD: Working-paper ground, a pale green-grey (#eef1ea) with sheets one step lighter; graphite ink; two pencil colours only, green for checked and red for exceptions, set on data, never on chrome. A condensed cut of one grotesk for titles and the footing figure, the regular cut for everything else, tabular figures throughout; a mono only for requests, responses and seals. Ticks are small drawn marks beside a value (traced ✓, agreed to sample S, near ≈, footed F, missing ✗) with a legend docked under the table. Totals sit on a double rule.

STORY: A non-developer with a spec watches Dapi read it, find the site's own data, write and test the extraction, and run it; rows arrive and are ticked as they land; they open any cell and see the server's own words, the capture time and a seal their browser re-checks; they see exactly what is missing and why.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the Dapi mark, Jobs and the job's name; Open run on Apify and New job at the right (near-black primary). Title row: job name at 24-30px condensed, source and row rule beneath; the footing figure "312 / 430" right-aligned at 36-40px with "issue rows delivered" under it. A six-step strip (spec, site, extraction, samples, run, footed), each with its tick and one line of result. Below, a sheet with filter and scope chips, the ticked table, a footing row on a double rule and the tick legend; to its right a 360px receipt column for the selected cell.

FORM: Audit Working Papers, #1 on the ordered grounded list (the pick card), chosen by the owner on 2026-10-31; seed:f0a946c47e34fe36.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
